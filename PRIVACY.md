# FileMaster Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 October 2026  
**Developer:** Ahmedbecetti  
**Contact:** ahmedbecetti41@gmail.com

FileMaster is an Android file-management and archive utility. This page describes how the current app works. The app's features and third-party SDK behavior can change as the app is updated.

## Files and folders

File browsing, copying, moving, renaming, archive creation/extraction, image previews, Trash, hidden-folder handling, and duplicate scanning are performed on the device using permissions granted by Android. FileMaster does not intentionally upload the contents of your selected files to a FileMaster-operated cloud service.

Android's Storage Access Framework controls which folders and documents the app can access. The app cannot bypass Android's protected-storage restrictions.

## Local-network sharing

If you explicitly start local-network sharing, FileMaster starts a temporary read-only HTTP server so other devices on the same network can download the selected files. Anyone who obtains the sharing URL while the server is running may be able to access those files. Stop sharing when finished and use trusted networks only. This feature is not end-to-end encrypted.

## Advertising and diagnostics

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, network information, ad interactions, and other data as described in Google's own policies and the consent choices available in your region. Internet speed testing is only initiated when you press the test button; it downloads up to approximately 1 MB from Cloudflare to estimate download throughput.

## App preferences and privacy lock

Language, theme, favorites, recent-file references, and the optional PIN-lock verifier are stored locally in app preferences. The PIN lock gates the app interface; it does not encrypt files on disk or replace Android's device lock. Hidden files are moved into a dot-prefixed folder and are not encrypted.

## Retention and deletion

Files remain under your Android storage permissions. App preferences can be cleared by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Items placed in the app-managed Trash remain in that folder until restored or permanently deleted by you.

## Children's privacy and changes

FileMaster is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. This policy may be updated alongside app changes. Please contact the developer with privacy questions.
